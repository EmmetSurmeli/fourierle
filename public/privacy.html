Privacy, in plain language.

Fourierle is a personal, non-commercial geography game. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking scripts. The game does not send your guesses, results, preferences, or practice history to its operator.

What stays in your browser

Fourierle uses local storage to remember daily guesses, results, difficulty, and appearance on this device. It uses session storage for practice games and practice scores in the current browser tab. These are needed to keep the game working as you play and return. The game code sets no cookies. You can remove this information at any time by clearing this site's browser storage; doing so resets your saved history and preferences.

What the host receives

When you open Fourierle, your browser requests the site and country files from Render. As with ordinary web hosting, Render may process your IP address, device or browser information, and requested URLs to deliver and protect the site. Render controls its own infrastructure data handling. The Fourierle operator does not receive your local game history from Render.

If you contact us

If you email ess256@cornell.edu, your email address and message will be used to read and respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. You can ask about or request deletion of correspondence you sent, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the game adds accounts, analytics, advertising, or other data practices. The update date above will change when the notice does.
