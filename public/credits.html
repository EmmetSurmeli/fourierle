Made from maps and mathematics.

The country outlines are derived from Natural Earth 1:50m data, which its authors place in the public domain. Fourierle simplifies some borders for play; the maps are not authoritative political boundaries.

Fourierle's original source code is released under the MIT license. The site also uses the following open-source components:
