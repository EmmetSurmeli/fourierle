Access to the game.

Fourierle supports keyboard country search, labeled controls, visible focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. You can pause the animation and keep the current outline visible.

Known limitation

The central puzzle asks players to identify a country from a visual canvas outline. Its current text alternative describes the drawing but cannot convey enough detail to provide an equivalent puzzle for someone who cannot see the shape. We are not claiming that the game is fully accessible to every player yet.

Tell us what is difficult

If a control or page is hard to use, or you need an alternative way to access information here, email ess256@cornell.edu. Please describe the page, browser or assistive technology, and problem if you can. We will use your feedback to improve the site.
